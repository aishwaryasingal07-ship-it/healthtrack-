💡 Why Reference Ranges Matter

Every pathology report prints a reference interval (minimum and maximum).

  • Min & Max: Tell the system whether your blood reading is safely within standard parameters.
  • Groq AI Engine: Uses these exact ranges to evaluate trends without guessing arbitrary laboratory standards.
  • History Graph: Renders healthy reference bounds so you can track your progress over time visually.
